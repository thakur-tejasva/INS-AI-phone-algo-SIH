General Inertial Navigation Systems.. well in principle.. its essentially.. since last known position/velocity.. it integrates translational acceleration ("(double integration of a (acceleration) wrt time (t))" error accumulated twice over time (Once for velocity change to get velocity) and then once again for displacement (delta s) but that leads to an error that scales in O(t^2) quadratic time due to the double integration due to any imprecision in acceleration.. due to the double integration which is fine as IMU(Inertial Measurement Units) are precise enough for the real life applications they are actually used for anyway... that is in missiles/ rockets. or ships/planes for the error rates to not compound anyway

But the default sensors in phones are not as precise as dedicated IMUs anyway.. also IMUs are well both more expensive and also not as readily available.. the solution is to calculate the velocity vector using an AI model over a period of preceding time and velocities.. that is a temporal AI model.. the type specifically we will use here is a Temporal Convolutional Network(TCN) which is essentially a type of time series Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)..

This way the network predicts velocity v directly and then "delta s=integration of v wrt time" and the errors come from imprecision in sensors (&other factor which may be random) but the overall error stays linear time O(t)....

Here despite its popularity... we don't use Transformer as though they are versatile and its possible.. this app is to be deployed on phones which most are not higher end supporting a so a TCN model would be trained on PyTorch on the dataset provided in google Colab and then for inference ability on mobile phones libraries like ExecuTorch((this is a successor of PyTorch mobile)for pytorch trained models running on android devices) will be used...

a last known position, orientation and velocity vectors will be taken.. since the GNSS(GPS, NaVIC, GLONASS etc..) outage... 

and then the output of this inference running locally on the mobile device.. will be used to get the velocity (& thus displacement to know the position)...
